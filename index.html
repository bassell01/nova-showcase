NOVA

Run your whole business in one place

NOVA is a multi-tenant business management platform: sales, invoicing, inventory, procurement, HR, payroll, projects and demand forecasting - in one clean, fast web app. English and Arabic, fully right-to-left.

Everything a small business needs

Sales flow, end to end

Customers, quotations, sales orders and multi-currency invoices with payment tracking.

Inventory & procurement

Live stock levels, low-stock alerts, CSV import and purchase orders that receive into stock.

HR & payroll

Employee directory, monthly payroll runs with configurable deduction brackets.

Projects

Track work with tasks, deadlines and progress per customer or internal project.

Ask NOVA

Ask questions in plain words and get answers computed from your data by a rule-based engine.

Demand forecast

A transparent statistical projection from your sales history. You can see exactly how every number is derived.

Multi-tenant & roles

Separate workspaces per company, role-based access, audit log, two-factor authentication.

English & Arabic

Full RTL layout with a one-click language toggle, including the guided onboarding tour.

Straight talk: NOVA is a finished, tested product - the automated test suite (backend and frontend) runs green in CI on every commit. The forecast is a documented statistical baseline, not a machine-learning model. The demo above runs on free hosting to keep evaluation costs at zero.
Built with Spring Boot, React and PostgreSQL. Docker image included; deploy anywhere in minutes.
